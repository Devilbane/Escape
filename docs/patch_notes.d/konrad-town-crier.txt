- Herald Konrad is a real town crier now: he climbs his new wooden podium on the square, raises a brass loudhailer and calls out the news in his tricorn of the crown. He announces your finished tasks and solved riddles, and reads the news every day at 10 am and 4 pm; a NEWS! sign beside the podium tells you when.
- Konrad steps out of Matthes's way when the street sweeper comes by with his broom, and thanks him for his work.
