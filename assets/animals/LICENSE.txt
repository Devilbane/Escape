The peaceful animals of the woods (between the graveyard and the village)

Deer.glb    "Deer" from "Ultimate Animated Animals" (July 2021) by Quaternius
Stag.glb    "Stag" from "Ultimate Animated Animals" (July 2021) by Quaternius
Fox.glb     "Fox" from "Ultimate Animated Animals" (July 2021) by Quaternius
Husky.glb, ShibaInu.glb, Cow.glb, Bull.glb   the same pack (the village's dogs, cows and bull)

The models were converted from glTF to binary glTF (.glb) for the game and
only the animations it plays were kept (Idle, Walk, Gallop, Eating).
Nothing else was changed.

Alpaca.glb additionally has a puffed-up, clumpy fleece (the torso and neck were
pushed outwards a little and given cream vertex colours with dark stockings) so
that it can serve as the village sheep. Its other animations were kept.
