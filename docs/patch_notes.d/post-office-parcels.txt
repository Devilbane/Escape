- The post office now has stamped and unstamped parcels and a sack of letters, and real letters in its pigeonholes instead of the glowing white blocks. The post office walls now carry post-themed notices: a royal horn emblem, postal services, post roads and a stamped-parcels notice.
- The post office counter now carries a ledger and quill, a parcel scale, a stamp and ink pad, sealing wax and a few letters.
