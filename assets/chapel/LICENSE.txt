Used here for the village temple (a small chapel): the wall, doorway, window
and rose window walls, the roof, the spire and the door, plus the trim sheet
(512 px), the stained glass and the door textures (scaled down to 256 px).
The trim sheet's stone blocks and roof tiles also dress the villagers'
half-timbered houses (see scripts/village_house.gd).
