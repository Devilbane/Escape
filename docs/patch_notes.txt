## Update of 10 November
- Animals (geese, goats, sheep, cows, dogs, deer, squirrels, hopping birds) keep out of walls and shut gates, wait and find another way like villagers, and stop their legs the moment they stop.
- Archivist Veth in the archive is a real boss now: ten times the life, a health bar, and a telegraphed purple shockwave every few seconds. Step out of the ring before it bursts.
- The armoured guardian now has 20 times a skeleton's life, so it can also be fought in normal combat (long and hard), while a stab in the back still kills it in one blow and it then never respawns.
- The attribute line now says how far each attribute is to its next point ("Speed 145% (87% to +1)") instead of a bare percentage.
- Ivy now climbs the bank's tower in branching vines, keeping clear of its door and windows.
- Base movement speed (Tempo, gear, gifts, level and companions together) is now capped at 150%; running still speeds you up on top of that cap, and the Tempo details and speed icon tell you when you are at the cap.
- The cap on base movement speed is raised from 150% to 175%.
- The temple's stained-glass windows shine brighter at night.
- The village now has a fenced cattle pen on its east meadow: the two cows and the bull live inside it and never wander off.
- Sharper new sounds: sword swings and hits, axe swings, the village bell, the anvil, rumbling stone, secrets, potions, bones and the heavier rain.
- Choosing "Sleep and log out" now saves and then closes the game.
- Copper coins in the house's coin chest now look like copper instead of gold.
- Fixed the recipe lists at the fine metalwork bench, the alchemist's table and the gem cutter's table: the ingredients now show on one readable line each instead of one letter per line.
- Flags and banners now flutter much stronger (about twice as far, and a bit livelier), in the village, the dungeon and the dragon's lair.
- The stone benches by the fountain no longer have red, blood-like stains; they look like worn, mossy stone.
- Glowworm swarms drift over the village and the open land at night, thickest in the village, and fade out with the dawn.
- The village goats are now proper goats with swept-back horns, a beard and natural walking, trotting and grazing; the red-horned goat has bigger red horns.
- Geese's wings are now attached to their bodies instead of floating beside them.
- The guild hall's ivy now spreads over every side, entrance included, as branching vines that keep windows and the door clear.
- The windows of the adventurers' guild hall are now free of ivy, with only a few strands at their edges.
- Spiders, the Spider Queen and the Hollow Mother now have a hairy, bristly coat: stiff pale-tipped hairs over dark fuzz, with a soft furry glow along their edges.
- A soft see-through circle now opens around the hero in the wall tops and walls that stand between the camera and the hero, so the hero and the floor around them are no longer hidden behind stone, above all in narrow corridors. The fog of war and the lighting stay as they were.
- Your own house now crackles softly with a cozy hearth fire instead of the cave air.
- The trophy wall in your house no longer gets squeezed in beside the front door where you could not reach it, and no banner or shield hangs behind a furniture spot there; it now hangs on a wall with free floor in front.
- Ivy now grows along the edges and corners of buildings and runs along the eaves, branching out from there.
- More ivy on the fronts of village buildings, most of all on the temple and the guild hall, and on the castle's gate and base; windows stay clear and doors stay recognisable.
- Gains in monster lore and skills now show as a brief word over your head ("Skeleton lore +5%", "Swordplay +1") instead of lines in the event log; the log keeps one explanation of monster lore.
- Monster lore only floats a word when it reaches a new whole percent.
- The stonemason now repairs a damaged village wall at night too, and goes back to bed once it is whole.
- The stonemason's wall repair now makes a soft, short stony tap instead of a harsh blow.
- Fixed the stonemason standing idle in a walking pose beside a hurt wall brick.
- Ivy keeps off the windmill's door and the doors of other stone buildings, covers the mill like other buildings, and is about 30% thinner everywhere, growing along vines without piled-up leaves.
- New sounds: all sword and axe blows, hits, swings and spells now use a combat sound pack, and the woodcutters' chopping and the village's church bell use new, richer recordings.
- Every place now has its own ambient soundscape: the village by day and night, the market chatter, the forge, the fields, campfires, the woods by day and night, light, steady and heavy rain, and a fitting loop on every dungeon floor (dungeon, cave, mine, crystal, fire, ancient halls, the spire). They fade into each other as you move.
- Ivy no longer grows over any door in the village: every entrance is swept clear of leaves.
- Villagers who bump into each other on the street now stop, face each other and chat for a few seconds before walking on.
- Tasks tied to a place (the bandit pass, the dungeon, Sternhall, the Ashen Spire) now show pinned at the top of the event log with a short line while you are there, until done. Brother Orlan in the archive gives a new one: bring him a healing potion.
- Pip and Pippi now really play: they chase each other across the meadow, tag each other and show off cartwheels and somersaults.
- Meet Pippi, Pip's red-haired friend with long pigtails and freckles: she minds the geese and goats with him, plays tag and chats with him in Lindenhain.
- Footsteps on grass, gravel, wooden floors, paving, paths and fields now use real recorded steps, each ground with its own sound.
- Steps into puddles now make a real, recorded splash, for deep and nearly dry puddles alike.
- A red-horned goat among Pip's goats now picks fights: it paws the ground, shouts, charges and bumps you (no damage). It cannot be hurt, gives up after 3 bumps and sulks for 10 minutes.
- The village sheep are now proper woolly sheep with a dark face and legs, short legs and a short neck.
- Slimes and blobs can now grow up to level 40 by eating and merging (they are still born no higher than level 25).
- Slimes and blobs now do 80% less damage to the village wall.
- Only slimes and blobs of level 20 or higher eat into the village wall now; smaller ones leave it alone.
- Slimes and Blobs that cannot reach a villager they see behind the village wall now eat into the wall instead of pushing against it.
- Squirrels now bound properly: stretched out in the air with paws forward and hind legs thrown back, tail arched high, and paws that alternate when they climb.
- Squirrels bound with a higher, longer leap, and their paws now hang on visible legs.
- Stairs now fit the place they stand in: the stairs out of bone, rock and crystal caves are hewn from the cave's own stone, and the shafts down in the mines are cut from rock, bone, obsidian, crystal or ember stone with uneven steps, a rough lip and faint glowing edges where the setting has them. Their steps are lit a little so they read as steps, not a black pit.
- The ruined courtyard of Sternhall now has a quiet outdoor wind-and-leaves ambience instead of the eerie hall drone.
- When the woodcutter helps the stonemason, the wall is mended 50% faster.
- Write "teleport" in the chat line to list every place with a 5-letter code, then write a code to be taken to that place's entrance.
- Branching ivy vines now spread across parts of the temple's roof, leaving the tiles showing between them.
- Townsfolk now make quiet footsteps that fit the ground they walk on, and splash in puddles; far away ones stay silent.
- Lindenhain now has animals: geese, goats, sheep, cows, a bull and two friendly dogs wander the village meadows, and Pip looks after the goats.
- Barrels, crates, planks, handcarts, stones and pumpkins in the village now sit in tidy heaps instead of overlapping and clipping into each other.
- Villagers no longer open their trade window during an alarm or away from their post; they say so instead.
- Villagers keep their swords and axes sheathed and only draw them when a slime or other danger threatens, putting them away again shortly after.
- Villagers can no longer walk through walls or shut gates: they wait and find another way round.
- The woodcutter now helps the stonemason repair the village wall.
- The woodcutter takes up a hammer and swings it when he helps mend the village wall.
- The stairs inside your house are now worn, well-used wooden stairs instead of grey stone.
- Squirrels bound about at the edge of the woods and run up the trees when you come near, and birds perch in the treetops and flutter off to another tree when you walk by.
- The village sheep are now woolly: a fluffy cream fleece with dark faces and legs, and they walk properly.
- Zarek the wandering merchant is a level 50 mage: he stays by his wagon and blasts every slime nearby with arcane bolts and fireballs, and can take a beating.

## Recent changes
- The wandering merchant's ingredients now show their own pictures (a mushroom, a tuft of moss, a blob of jelly ...) instead of a backpack.
- Village ivy now grows on every side of a building (each side as thick as the front used to be), and it never fully covers a window any more, including on the stone buildings.
- The village warning bell now uses a real recording of church bells ringing in a tower.
- The bookshelf in your house now stands on the ground floor against the north wall, and the wall torch beside it hangs one wall to the west.
- Rain now falls only a third as often; fog, storms and clear skies take its place, and each spell of rain lasts as long as before.
- Fixed being shut in the archive's bed room after sleeping there: the iron bars of the prisoner's cell no longer count as a way out, so the secret wall opens from inside.
- You can use at most 9 shrines in the whole beginner dungeon; the shrine window shows how many blessings are left, and after the ninth the shrines go quiet.
- Prison cells now really hold: the iron bars of a cell are turned with its wall, so a column of bars is solid from end to end and nobody walks through the gaps between the tiles.
- The dark fill around the dungeon no longer covers the floor grates or the stairs going down: you see the pits below the grates again, and the stairs are open.
- The launcher now shows only the newest two sections of the patch notes, so old notes no longer need trimming.
- A slime you hit now turns on you first: it drops villagers and the wall as targets while you fight it, and goes back to them after about 6 seconds without a blow or when you get far away.
- Much more ivy in the village, always clear of doors: thick on the guild hall, castle and temple, a little on the fountain.
- Stepping out of the crypt now starts a two-minute run to the village: nothing can hurt you (blows, traps, poison, bleeding, fire) while the shield countdown shows, and it ends early when you reach the village.
- The plain, unlit timber frames in the mine tunnels (bare brown beams with no texture or lighting) are gone.
- A backpack you lost (your bag after being carried off) or set down on the floor now shows on the map as an orange backpack mark, with "Lost backpack" in the legend. The mark is gone once the pack is picked up, emptied or swept away.
- Bone Pit (level 8): the visible spike traps now sit on the same bone-coloured floor as the rest, the rough floor patches match it, and the skulls and bones in the walls no longer shimmer or hang in the air when a wall fades out as you walk by.
- Weak monsters now ignore you when their level is below half your level plus 2: they no longer notice, chase or attack you, but still defend themselves if you hit them. Bosses always fight.
- Crates and stone blocks are no longer dropped in front of you: they are always set down right under your feet and you end up standing on top of them, so you can stack up and step off as usual.
- The faint "db" watermark in the bottom-right corner now shows the new db logo, with the same size and transparency.
- The logo above the title on the play screen is gone; it stays on the launcher's loading screen.
- The dark void around every dungeon is now filled with a plain, dark version of the dungeon's own wall material (stone, bone, rock, obsidian or crystal), and the wall tops and outer sides are plain and flat instead of busy, so no black gap shows, not even beside a hidden secret passage. Nothing else changes.
- Stairs going down now open towards the open floor of the room instead of lining up with a wall.
- A secret room can always be opened from the inside: if a loaded game or a night slept in its bed leaves you behind a closed secret wall, the wall slides open by itself.
- A great Norse horn now stands inside the village wall, right beside the west gate. When the village is alarmed the town guard walks to it and blows two long, deep blasts like a ship's fog horn; the church bell tolls once the horn has died away, so the two never sound over each other.
- A classic church bell now tolls over the village when something hostile happens: a slime at the gate or the wall, a broken stretch of wall, a slime inside, or a villager eaten. It rings at most once every 30 seconds.
- The compendium has a new Places page with the description of every floor you have entered, so you can read it again after it fades from the screen.
- The Windows download is now the main release: every update of the game builds it automatically, and the browser version is only published on request.
- The village stonemason now mends the wall at 4 HP a second and never tires: there is no daily limit on his work any more.
- Dungeon walls now have tops and outer sides in their own stone, bone, rock, obsidian or crystal look, so no black void shows right beside them. Nothing else changes: walls block you exactly as before.
- Slimes now go for villagers first: a slime with a way to reach a villager hunts them instead of eating into the village wall, and only attacks the wall when no villager is within reach.
- Your house now has a bookshelf in the cellar: put your books on it and the shelf shows how many you keep, with a count on its plaque.
- Pointing at an item in your bag, a chest or the vault now compares it with what you wear in that place: green numbers where it is better, red where it is worse.
- A book now teaches for 100 hours of reading, then it is used up: it stays in your bag or chest, but you learn nothing more from it. Its details and the day plan show the hours left.
- New cave monsters: fire elementals, obsidian, stone and crystal golems, each with telegraphed special moves (fire bolts and scorched floor, shard throws, quakes, a mirror ward and a prismatic burst).
- Cracks now spread over the stones of the village wall as slimes eat into it, in three clear stages (light, medium, heavy), so you can see how close a brick is to breaking.
- The town guard now checks the west gate for slimes much more often at night and in or after rain, and calls the alarm when he spots one.
- Every slime outside the village now eats into the village wall, and at least one attempt in three breaks a stretch of it.
- The event log has a slim scroll bar you can drag to read older messages.
- New cave looks for walls and floors: bone (with skulls and bones), rock, obsidian and glowing crystal, in three seamless variants each, with matching torches, braziers and candelabra. Bone Pit, Bone Gallery, Copper Mine, Crystal Grotto and Ember Tunnels wear them.
- Forging at Gorm's forge is now a d100 roll: your chance rises with Strength, Intelligence, Intuition, Dexterity and your forging skill. A failed attempt spoils half the bars, but you still learn.
- Sneaking no longer makes the hero strike back on their own; only a foe you click is attacked.
- The hoard at home now counts the coins you carry plus those in your chest, and your bank vault shows its own hoard of the coins stored there.
- Stairs down are now real stone steps that fade into the dark, in houses, mines and the dragon's lair, instead of a black hole.
- The glowing ember cracks in the deep mines can be crossed now, but they burn: 1 life a second near them, 3 while standing on one.
- Ghostly voices (moans, a banshee's shriek, the whispers out of the holes) and bandit taunts now also appear in the event log.
- Falling rocks in Sternhall's vaults hit harder: a direct hit now takes about a quarter of your life.
- New dungeon level: the Great Old One's lair (level 25) with traps, secret rooms, bones and a spread-out hoard.
- Townsfolk now hold the village wall against slimes until it breaks, and the wall sits in the narrow neck by the village.

## Earlier this week
- Smaller touches: knives refill from the belt, purses count up into silver and gold, a star rings a vital blow, splashier puddles, steadier moon shadows, a chat line under the event log and health bars with levels on slimes and big monsters.
- A cape wardrobe in the house, a new app icon and aged entrances for the mine, the Great Old One's lair and the Ashen Spire.
- The village is built of aged stone with ivy and paved paths; nights can be nearly pitch black and moonlight changes with the moon's phase. Light your torch with V.
- A new HUD: life bar with condition icons, ammo under it, gold under the XP bar, messages over the hero's head and a scrollable event log.
- Combat: hit and block chances on the character page, thunder weapons that can stun, and a short regeneration pause after a blow.
- Walk or run with a stamina meter; a running hero hit hard gets knocked down; sneaking turns you grey and see-through.
- A 20-slot backpack, several save games, a storage chest in the mountain tavern and the new map workshop for building your own levels.
