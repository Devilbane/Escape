Footsteps (step_0 to step_5): "Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone)",
the stone steps, recorded by Fantozzi (freesound.org/people/Fantozzi),
https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
License: CC0 (public domain).

Sneaking steps (sneak_0 to sneak_3): the cloth steps from
"Footsteps Leather, Cloth, Armor",
https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-leather-cloth-armor
License: CC0 (public domain).

Door (door): qubodup-DoorOpen07 from "Door Open, Door Close Set" by qubodup,
https://opengameart.org/content/door-open-door-close-set
License: CC0 (public domain).

eerie_0..5.wav (far-off howls, roars and screams, pitched down, muffled and
given a cave echo), chain.wav (door chains) and the skeleton voices
(voice_<kind>_0 and _1, pitched, with a bone rattle) are made from
"80 CC0 creature SFX" and "80 CC0 RPG SFX" by rubberduck, CC0:
https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-creature-sfx
https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx
License: CC0 (public domain).

Archer shots (bow_0 to bow_2): "Crossbow Shoot" and "Crossbow Bolt Pass" from
"Medieval sound effects - Weapon Textures", layered and trimmed,
https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-sound-effects-weapon-textures
License: CC0 (public domain).

The hero's cries for the comic words (voice_aua, voice_uff, voice_autsch,
voice_au) are spoken by the Piper speech synthesizer with the "thorsten_emotional"
voice (angry), built by tools/make_voices.py. Voice data: Thorsten-Voice by
Thorsten Müller, https://github.com/thorstenMueller/Thorsten-Voice
License: CC0 (public domain).

All other sounds, including the comic word noises (word_*), are made by
tools/make_sounds.py.
