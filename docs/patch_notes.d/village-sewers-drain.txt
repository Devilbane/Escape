- A little stream now runs from the valley river up the pass beside the road and dives under the village through an iron grating. Down below, the village sewers (brick tunnels with a slow, murky channel carrying leaves and rubbish) run to an outfall in the north-west, where the water flows out into a river in the forest. Climb down through the manhole hatches (run into the lid to heave it open) and look through the street drains.
