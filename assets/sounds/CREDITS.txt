Footsteps (step_0 to step_5): "Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone)",
the stone steps, recorded by Fantozzi (freesound.org/people/Fantozzi),
https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
License: CC0 (public domain).
The steps on sand and soil from the same pack (step_dirt_0 to step_dirt_5)
are the paths, dirt floors and soil. The steps on grass, scree and wooden
floors (step_grass_*, step_scree_*, step_wood_*) are made from these
recordings by tools/make_ground_steps.py.

License: CC0 (public domain).

The eerie dungeon sounds (dungeon_drip_*, dungeon_moan_*, dungeon_chains_*,
dungeon_whisper_*, dungeon_creak_*, dungeon_wind_*, dungeon_skitter_*) are made

The windmill's sails in the wind and its groaning wood (windmill_whoosh_*,
windmill_groan_*) are made from scratch by tools/make_windmill_sounds.py.

The fanfares for loot finer than rare (loot_epic, loot_godborn) are made
from scratch by tools/make_rarity_sounds.py.

The splashes of a step into a rain puddle (splash_0 to splash_3) are four
single steps cut from "Running Through Puddle" by soundza (soundza.com), trimmed,
compressed a little, levelled to match the other footsteps and faded at the
ends. The earlier splashes, made from scratch by tools/make_weather_sounds.py
like the rain and the thunder, stay in the project's asset library as an
alternative (puddle-sound/old). The soft squelches (splash_soft_*) are still
made by that tool. Do not run the tool for splash_0 to 3 or it overwrites these.

The village's warning bell (village_bell) is a 9.5 second cut of "Church_Bells_Loop_01"
from "Fantasy Ambience Sounds Pack V2", mixed down to mono, resampled to 22.05 kHz and
faded in and out (tools/make_pack_sounds.py). It plays once per alarm, so it is not
looped. (tools/make_bell_sounds.py, the old synthesised bell, is no longer used.)

The guard's alarm horn (war_horn) is made by tools/make_horn_sounds.py;
it is synthesised from scratch, nothing is downloaded.

Bought sound packs, built by tools/make_pack_sounds.py (each file keeps the name of the
sound it replaced):
- "Anime Combat Sounds" by The Sound Guild: all the sword and axe blows (sword_*, axe_*:
  swings, sweeps, plain, heavy, vital, sneak and glancing hits), and the generic hit,
  swing, throw, hurt and magic sounds. Cut to the blow, mono, faded.
- "Fantasy Ambience Sounds Pack V2": the woodcutter's chop (chop, from
  Chopping_Wood_Without_Whoosh_Loop_01), the village bell (village_bell, from
  Church_Bells_Loop_01), the rain (rain.ogg, from Rain_Medium_Loop_01) and the village at
  night (village_night.ogg, from Village_Night_Loop_01, played outdoors at night).
The packs were bought by the project owner; the licence terms are those of the sellers.
The ambient soundscapes (rain, rain_light, rain_heavy, village_night, amb_*.ogg, played by
scripts/soundscape.gd) are loops of "Fantasy Ambience Sounds Pack V2", brought to one loudness,
mono, 24 kHz (tools/make_pack_sounds.py). Which loop plays where is listed in docs/sound_replacements.md.
