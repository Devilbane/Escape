- Meet Wolfram the Climber on the mountainside east of the stream: he climbs the rock all day with hammer, nails and rope, rests at the top to enjoy the view, and climbs back down taking everything with him.
- Ask him about "climbing" or "trade" to buy climbing gear (helmet, shoes, gloves, hammer, rope, nails): he has only a little, for more than Mira asks. Watch him climb for an hour and you get a rough idea of it. Sleep on it before you try for real.
