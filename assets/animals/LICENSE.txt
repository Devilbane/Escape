The peaceful animals of the woods (between the graveyard and the village)

Deer.glb    "Deer" from "Ultimate Animated Animals" (July 2021) by Quaternius
Stag.glb    "Stag" from "Ultimate Animated Animals" (July 2021) by Quaternius
Fox.glb     "Fox" from "Ultimate Animated Animals" (July 2021) by Quaternius
Husky.glb, Cow.glb, Bull.glb   the same pack (the village's dogs, cows and bull)

https://quaternius.com
License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0), public domain.
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The models were converted from glTF to binary glTF (.glb) for the game and
only the animations it plays were kept (Idle, Walk, Gallop, Eating).
Nothing else was changed.

Sheep.glb   "Sheep" from "Farm Animals Pack" (2018) by Quaternius, also CC0
            (https://opengameart.org/content/lowpoly-animated-farm-animal-pack).
            The pack's sheep only has an Idle and a Jump; its Walk, Gallop and Eating
            were made for the game by turning the bones (tools/build_sheep.gd).

Rabbit.glb  "Rabbit" by CDmir / Cestmir Dammer (kelgar.org), CC0, from
            https://opengameart.org/content/rabbit-0 (rabbit-FBX.7z). Built with tools/build_rabbit.gd:
            the fur shell mesh was dropped, the skin shrunk to 512 px, the model scaled to the size of
            the other animals, and only Idle (its alert pose), Walk (a hop), Gallop (its run) and
            Eating (sitting) were kept. The baby rabbits of the warren are the same model, smaller.

Donkey.glb  "Donkey" from "Ultimate Animated Animals" (July 2021) by Quaternius, CC0 like the
            others (the wandering merchant's donkey). Converted to .glb the same way, keeping
            only Idle, Idle_Headlow and Eating.
